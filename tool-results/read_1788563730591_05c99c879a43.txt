    72→# 3. UX Design Principles
    73→
    74→FayaNMS follows eight principles.
    75→
    76→### 1. Operational clarity
    77→
    78→Users must immediately understand:
    79→
    80→- what is healthy
    81→- what is unhealthy
    82→- what changed
    83→- what requires action
    84→- what is risky
    85→
    86→### 2. High information density
    87→
    88→Network engineers routinely work with:
    89→
    90→- hundreds of devices
    91→- thousands of interfaces
    92→- configuration lines
    93→- large event streams
    94→- many metrics
    95→
    96→Avoid oversized consumer-SaaS layouts.
    97→
    98→### 3. Progressive disclosure
    99→
   100→Show:
   101→
   102→```text
   103→Summary
   104→   ↓
   105→Important information
   106→   ↓
   107→Detailed information
   108→   ↓
   109→Raw technical information
   110→```
   111→
   112→### 4. Safety before convenience
   113→
   114→Destructive actions must clearly communicate:
   115→
   116→- device
   117→- scope
   118→- effect
   119→- risk
   120→- validation
   121→- rollback capability
   122→
   123→### 5. Consistency
   124→
   125→One pattern for:
   126→
   127→- tables
   128→- statuses
   129→- filters
   130→- forms
   131→- side panels
   132→- confirmations
   133→- audit records
   134→
   135→### 6. Evidence-driven operations
   136→
   137→Important actions should link to:
   138→
   139→- snapshots
   140→- audit records
   141→- changes
   142→- incidents
   143→- logs
   144→- metrics
   145→
   146→### 7. Accessibility
   147→
   148→Target:
   149→
   150→**WCAG 2.2 AA**
   151→
   152→### 8. Keyboard-first operations
   153→
   154→Power users should be able to navigate without excessive mouse interaction.
