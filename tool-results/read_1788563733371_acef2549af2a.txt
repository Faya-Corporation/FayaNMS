    72→    72→# 3. UX Design Principles
    73→    73→
    74→    74→FayaNMS follows eight principles.
    75→    75→
    76→    76→### 1. Operational clarity
    77→    77→
    78→    78→Users must immediately understand:
    79→    79→
    80→    80→- what is healthy
    81→    81→- what is unhealthy
    82→    82→- what changed
    83→    83→- what requires action
    84→    84→- what is risky
    85→    85→
    86→    86→### 2. High information density
    87→    87→
    88→    88→Network engineers routinely work with:
    89→    89→
    90→    90→- hundreds of devices
    91→    91→- thousands of interfaces
    92→    92→- configuration lines
    93→    93→- large event streams
    94→    94→- many metrics
    95→    95→
    96→    96→Avoid oversized consumer-SaaS layouts.
    97→    97→
    98→    98→### 3. Progressive disclosure
    99→    99→
   100→   100→Show:
   101→   101→
   102→   102→```text
   103→   103→Summary
   104→   104→   ↓
   105→   105→Important information
   106→   106→   ↓
   107→   107→Detailed information
   108→   108→   ↓
   109→   109→Raw technical information
   110→   110→```
   111→   111→
   112→   112→### 4. Safety before convenience
   113→   113→
   114→   114→Destructive actions must clearly communicate:
   115→   115→
   116→   116→- device
   117→   117→- scope
   118→   118→- effect
   119→   119→- risk
   120→   120→- validation
   121→   121→- rollback capability
   122→   122→
   123→   123→### 5. Consistency
   124→   124→
   125→   125→One pattern for:
   126→   126→
   127→   127→- tables
   128→   128→- statuses
   129→   129→- filters
   130→   130→- forms
   131→   131→- side panels
   132→   132→- confirmations
   133→   133→- audit records
   134→   134→
   135→   135→### 6. Evidence-driven operations
   136→   136→
   137→   137→Important actions should link to:
   138→   138→
   139→   139→- snapshots
   140→   140→- audit records
   141→   141→- changes
   142→   142→- incidents
   143→   143→- logs
   144→   144→- metrics
   145→   145→
   146→   146→### 7. Accessibility
   147→   147→
   148→   148→Target:
   149→   149→
   150→   150→**WCAG 2.2 AA**
   151→   151→
   152→   152→### 8. Keyboard-first operations
   153→   153→
   154→   154→Power users should be able to navigate without excessive mouse interaction.
   155→   155→

   630→   630→

   632→   632→
   633→   633→```text

   644→   644→

   652→   652→

   660→   660→- badges for important counts
